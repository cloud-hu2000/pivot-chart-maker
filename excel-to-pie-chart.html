Excel to Pie Chart Maker

Create a pie chart from Excel or CSV data when you need to show a small number of category shares. Upload a clean spreadsheet, select the category and value fields, and inspect the percentage breakdown before exporting the chart.

Show a concise share of the total

A pie chart works best when the categories are few and clearly different, such as budget allocation across three departments. It is less useful for a long list of groups or nearly equal values; in those situations, a bar chart makes comparisons easier.

Start with an XLSX or CSV file that has clear headers and one record per row. The tool processes data locally in your browser and can calculate a sum, average, or count to produce a share-of-total view.

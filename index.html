Create a chart you can explain

A useful pivot chart begins with a question, not a chart type. Decide whether you need a category comparison, a change over time, or a small share-of-total view. Then prepare a tidy table with one header row and one observation per row. Clear headers such as Date, Region, Category, Sales, and Quantity make it easier to select the right fields and check the result later.

Build the pivot table before relying on the chart. Use Sum for a total, Count for the number of records, and Average for a typical value. Compare several pivot-table cells and the grand total with the source file, especially after applying a filter. A bar, line, or pie chart should make a checked result easier to understand; it should not hide the calculation behind it.

What the built-in sales sample demonstrates

The sample dataset contains twelve deliberately inspectable records: four regions crossed with three product categories. Configure Region as the row field, Category as the column field, Sales as the value, and Sum as the calculation. The resulting grand total is $1,194,082. West has the highest regional total at $404,815, while Technology is the largest category across all regions at $528,627. These figures are not claims about a real company; they are fixed practice data designed so every number can be traced back to a source row.

Use that traceability as a review exercise. Add East's three category values and confirm its $333,187 row total. Then compare the four Technology records with the category total. Finally, switch the value field from Sales to Quantity or change Sum to Average and state how the question changed. A chart is reliable only when its grouping, measure, calculation, and filters are all understood.

With your own data, keep the same discipline: record the reporting period, define the measure, check several cells, and preserve the summarized table with the image. This produces an analysis that a colleague can reproduce instead of a graphic that has lost its calculation context. You can also download the sample CSV or follow the complete worked example.

CSV to Pie Chart Maker

Create a pie chart from CSV data when you need to show how a small number of categories contribute to one total. Upload a clean file, select a category and value, then verify that the shares add up before exporting the chart.

Use a pie chart for a clear part-to-whole question

Pie charts are most readable when there are only a few categories and the total matters. They can show the share of support requests by type, for example. When the task is comparing very similar values or a long list of groups, a bar chart is usually clearer.

Prepare the CSV with descriptive headers and one record per row. The tool summarizes values locally in your browser, allowing you to choose the calculation that fits the data without sending the file to a server.

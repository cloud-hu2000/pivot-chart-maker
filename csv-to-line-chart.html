CSV to Line Chart Maker

Create a line chart from CSV data when your main question is about change over time. Upload a file with a date or time field, select the numeric values to summarize, and review the result before you export the chart.

Show a trend with a well-ordered time series

Line charts are useful for monthly revenue, daily traffic, or other ordered measurements. Use a consistent date format and group records by a meaningful interval such as day, month, or quarter. A clear interval makes it easier to distinguish a real trend from normal variation.

Keep the source CSV tidy: one header row, one observation per row, and numbers stored as numbers. The browser tool can then calculate sums, averages, or counts without uploading the spreadsheet to a server.

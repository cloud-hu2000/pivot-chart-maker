Privacy Policy

How Pivot Chart Maker handles data, cookies, and advertising

Effective date: August 5, 2026. This policy describes the information practices of the independent Pivot Chart Maker website.

Summary

Pivot Chart Maker is a browser-based spreadsheet tool and educational website. The tool is designed to process the CSV and XLSX files you choose locally in your browser. The site does not ask visitors to create an account, and it is not designed to transmit the contents of an uploaded spreadsheet to a Pivot Chart Maker analysis server. Like most websites, the hosting provider may process limited technical information needed to deliver and secure the site. If third-party advertising is enabled, advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers as described below.

Spreadsheet files and tool data

When you select a CSV or XLSX file in the tool, the browser reads the file so it can display a preview, calculate a pivot table, render a chart, and create downloads. The working data is held in browser memory for the active page session. Refreshing or closing the page clears that active working session. Exports such as PNG, CSV, and XLSX are generated on your device. Because web browsers and extensions vary, you should avoid using any online tool with highly sensitive data unless you have evaluated it for your own requirements.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, the hosting and security services that deliver the site may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device and operating-system information, referring page, requested URL, and the date and time of the request. This information may be recorded in server or security logs to operate the website, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and understand basic traffic. Pivot Chart Maker does not currently provide a user-account area or intentionally collect names, addresses, or payment details through the site.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files that a website or service can store in a browser. The core Pivot Chart Maker tool does not need a cookie to parse your spreadsheet locally. However, hosting, security, embedded services, analytics, or advertising technologies may use cookies, local storage, pixels, or similar identifiers when they are present. Browser settings can usually be used to block, delete, or limit cookies, although doing so can affect features on some sites.

Third-party advertising and Google

Pivot Chart Maker may display third-party advertising in the future, including advertisements served by Google. When ads are displayed, Google and other advertising providers may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown, detect fraud, and, where allowed, show ads based on a visitor's interests or prior visits to this and other websites. This policy is published so that visitors understand that relationship before such advertising is enabled.

Visitors can learn about Google's advertising technologies and controls in Google's advertising policy and may manage ad personalization through My Ad Center. Where legally required, the site will request consent before using non-essential cookies or personalized advertising technologies. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland should be shown an appropriate consent choice before personalized ads are served.

How information is used and shared

Technical information may be used to run, secure, improve, and measure the website, respond to messages, and comply with legal obligations. Information may be processed by service providers that host, secure, or support the website, and by advertising partners when advertising is enabled. Pivot Chart Maker does not sell uploaded spreadsheet contents. The site does not knowingly use sensitive categories such as health, religion, or sexual orientation to build advertising audiences, and it does not intentionally send email addresses, phone numbers, or other directly identifying information in advertising requests.

Your choices and rights

You can avoid providing a spreadsheet by using the sample data, clear your active local session by refreshing the page, and control cookies through your browser. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about personal data related to you. To make a privacy request or ask a question about this policy, email cloudhu2000@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify and process a request, and some data may be retained when required for security, legal, or recordkeeping reasons.

Children and policy updates

Pivot Chart Maker is a general-audience productivity and educational website; it is not directed to children. If this policy changes materially, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the website after an update is subject to the revised policy.

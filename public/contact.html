Get in touch with Pivot Chart Maker

Questions, accessibility feedback, privacy requests, and reproducible bug reports are welcome.

Email the site operator

For support or feedback, email cloudhu2000@gmail.com. This is the contact address for the independent operator of Pivot Chart Maker. Please include the page URL, browser and device type, and a short description of what happened when reporting a technical issue. That information makes it much easier to investigate a problem without asking for your full spreadsheet.

Privacy and data requests

Use the same email address for questions about this policy, cookies, advertising disclosures, or a request related to personal information. The browser tool is designed to process uploaded CSV and XLSX files locally, so the site operator ordinarily does not receive the contents of those files. Do not email sensitive, confidential, or personally identifiable spreadsheet data unless it is essential and you are authorized to share it.

What to expect

Messages are reviewed by the site operator. A reply may not be immediate, but legitimate support and privacy questions are handled as reasonably as possible. Pivot Chart Maker does not offer paid consulting, account support for third-party products, or a guarantee that every spreadsheet can be parsed. When a file has unusual formatting, the guides can help identify a clean, table-shaped version that is more likely to work.

Before reporting a chart issue

Check that the source has one header row, one record per row, numeric values in the selected value field, and no pre-calculated totals. Those four checks solve many unexpected pivot results.
